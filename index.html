ABOUT JAMES

Animal Science with a vision beyond the classroom.

I am James Kamangala, a fourth-year BSc Animal Science student at Palabana University with a growing professional focus on climate change, animal production, research and agribusiness.

My interest is in finding practical, evidence-based ways to make livestock systems more productive, resilient and profitable—especially where farmers face constraints in feed, water, technology and resources.

THE BIG QUESTION

How do we keep livestock productive as the climate changes?

Climate change is not only an environmental issue. It affects feed availability, water, heat stress, disease pressure, productivity and farm profitability. This section will focus on practical, evidence-based approaches to building more resilient livestock systems.

“Resilient livestock production starts with solutions that farmers can actually use.”
