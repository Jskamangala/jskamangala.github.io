THE BIG QUESTION

How do we keep livestock productive as the climate changes?

Climate change is not only an environmental issue. It affects feed availability, water, heat stress, disease pressure, productivity and farm profitability. This section will focus on practical, evidence-based approaches to building more resilient livestock systems.

“Resilient livestock production starts with solutions that farmers can actually use.”
